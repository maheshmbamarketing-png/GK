Crafted with care

STRUCTURE

  • RCC framed structure with seismic design
  • All Walls Constructed in Wire cut Chamber Red Bricks.

FLOORING

  • 2' × 2' vitrified tile flooring in living, dining and all bedrooms
  • Matt-finish ceramic tile flooring in kitchen
  • Anti-skid ceramic tile flooring in balconies and bathrooms

WALL FINISHES

  • External and internal walls with cement plastering
  • Exterior-grade emulsion paint for outer walls
  • Putty finish and quality emulsion paint for interior walls
  • Enamel paint for MS grills and shutters

MAIN DOOR

  • Teak wood door frame and teak wood panels with melamine polish
  • Godrej mortise lock

OTHER DOORS

  • Hard-wood door frame
  • Readymade flush shutters

WINDOWS

  • UPVC windows
  • Ventilator system with translucent glass for toilets

KITCHEN

  • 2' high ceramic tile dado
  • Granite counter top
  • Stainless steel single bowl sink
  • Provision for Aqua-guard point and microwave
  • Loft provided for kitchen

TOILETS

  • Anti-skid flooring and tile dado up to 7 ft height
  • Wash basin and wall-mounted water closet of ESSCO / PARRYWARE
  • Quality CP fittings of ESSCO / PARRYWARE
  • Provision for geyser

PLUMBING

  • Concealed water supply in CPVC FlowGuard material

  • Individual Bore for Tower-A and Toer-B with 10000 Litres capacity Water Tank

ELECTRICAL

  • Concealed internal wiring in PVC conduits
  • ISI standard cables of RR Kabel or equivalent
  • Modular switches of Legrand or equivalent
  • Three phase supply for each 2, 3 & 4 bedroom flat

  • EV Charger available in Stilt Floor
